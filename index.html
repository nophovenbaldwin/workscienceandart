TWIN CITIES PLASTER & DRYWALL SOLUTIONS



Work Science and Art is a plastering and carpentry business serving the Twin Cities.

I specialize in traditional and decorative plasterwork, wall repair, and drywall finishing. My work includes historic plaster restoration, repairing cracks and delamination, patching and repairing walls after plumbing, electrical work, or other damage, traditional lime and veneer plastering, and detail-oriented painting.

I also offer finish carpentry, window restoration, and restoration carpentry.

My business is a one-man operation and I handle all the details from start to finish.
